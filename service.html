Our Services

Services Built Specifically For Your Business

Corporate Antivirus

With the rise of mobile and remote work, businesses must secure corporate data both inside and beyond traditional perimeters. We provide enterprise-wide virus protection and content security throughout the entire threat lifecycle. Our solutions are high-performance, centrally managed, and fully scalable, supporting enterprises and desktops alike. While our core antivirus solution is KASPERSKY, we also offer E-set node32, Sophos, and Bitdefender.

Data Loss Prevention

Businesses face risks from both external end users—such as customers or contractors who may inadvertently introduce threats like phishing, spyware, and viruses—and internal users, employees who, whether accidentally or maliciously, can compromise client data, intellectual property, and strategic plans. Our solutions, including Cyberoam, Sophos, Cisco, and Checkpoint, classify and protect sensitive information, monitor and control endpoints, and provide identity-based access controls with comprehensive logging. Cost-effective and tailored to each organization, our Unified Threat Management appliances streamline security management, ensuring robust protection against all potential threats.

Laptop Encryption

Laptops are easily lost or stolen, putting confidential data at risk—especially for mobile senior managers who rely on them as primary workstations. The exposure of unsecured laptops can result in millions in losses, damaging customer relationships, business partnerships, and corporate reputation. Checkpoint’s Pointsec laptop security software provides strong, user-friendly encryption, simplifying deployment and management for laptops, desktops, and mobile devices while offering cost-effective protection for corporate environments.

IP Telephony & VOIP Networks

Effective communication is essential for any organization’s success. At Oleander Solutions, we ensure you stay connected by partnering with industry leaders like Cisco, D-Link, Yealink, Zycoo, and Panasonic to provide reliable, affordable, and cutting-edge communication solutions. With IP telephony and VoIP, organizations can enhance connectivity and efficiency, embracing the latest technology trends to optimize their communication infrastructure.

Surveillance Systems

Whether at home or in the office, safeguarding your investments is a priority. Oleander Solutions provides advanced surveillance systems that let you monitor every corner of your environment, giving you peace of mind to focus on what matters. We offer both indoor and outdoor solutions, allowing you to supervise employees, domestic staff, or multiple areas of your home or workplace efficiently and reliably.

IT Related Supplies

We deal in a wide range of IT related supplies; printers, laptops, telephone headsets etc. And to make sure that we do this in compliance with the market standards, we have taken the initiative of creating strategic partnerships with various industry leading brands like HP, DELL, LENOVO, MICROSOFT, RUCKUS, AVAYA, PANASONIC etc.

A privately-held company in Nairobi, the firm specializes in Information Security, Network Solutions, Telephony services, IT consulting and Surveillance System.
